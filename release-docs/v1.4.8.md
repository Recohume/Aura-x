# Aura v1.4.8

Release Tags: [release, v1.4.8, android, kotlin, jetpack-compose, local-recommendations, personalized-home, lyrics-motion, categories]

---

## Overview

Aura v1.4.8 makes Home feel more personal without sending a listening profile to a server. Quick Picks, music videos, albums, and liked-song shelves are ranked on the device from the listener's saved songs and playback activity. Home category pills now change the candidate content across the page, while the lyrics/player experience gets a more continuous album-art transition and a clearer Romaji/Original control.

This is a native Android release built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Recommendation ranking uses a transparent local scoring function; it does not embed Python, call a recommendation backend, or download a machine-learning model.

## Highlights

### 1. Personalized Home Recommendations

- **Quick Picks use the listener's taste profile**. Candidate tracks are scored against artists, genres, and album metadata represented in Liked Songs and Recently Played.
- **Playback feedback influences ranking**. Aura records track starts, natural completions, and deliberate next-track skips on the current device. Favorites are also a strong positive signal.
- **Recency and repeat engagement matter**. More recent listening contributes more to the taste profile, while repeated engagement can strengthen a track's artist/style affinity. Repeated recent tracks receive a small freshness penalty so Quick Picks can introduce adjacent tracks.
- **Artist variety is preserved**. The local ranker applies a diversity pass that caps an artist's initial appearance count before filling the remainder of a shelf.
- **Music videos are ranked separately**. Category or artist-related video candidates receive a preference score, with official-video wording favored and cover, karaoke, reaction, nightcore, slowed, lyric-only, or fan-made wording down-ranked.
- **Albums for You is personalized** using album-search candidates and album metadata found in track results. When album metadata is incomplete, the result can use the candidate title as the displayed album name.
- **Liked Songs influence recommendations** and are also surfaced in a Home shelf. When a category is selected, the shelf is limited to liked tracks related to that category's candidates.

### 2. Category Selection Updates Home Content

The Home pills—**All**, **New Release**, **Trending**, **Top Chart**, and **Podcasts**—now change the content used by the main recommendation shelves rather than only changing the bottom track list.

- Quick Picks and the track list use candidates for the active category.
- Music videos are fetched and ranked for the active category.
- Album candidates are refreshed and ranked for the active category when applicable.
- Liked songs, recent tracks, artists, and saved playlists are filtered for category relevance so unrelated shelves do not keep showing the same content.
- Podcasts use video/episode candidates; the album shelf is omitted when album suggestions do not apply.
- Selecting **All** returns to the general personalized Home shelves.

Category candidate retrieval uses YouTube Music search and therefore needs internet access for fresh catalog items. The listening profile and final ranking remain local.

### 3. Home Layout and Library Playlists

- Restored the time-aware greeting at the top of Home, with the category pills directly beneath it.
- Quick Picks displays four compact track rows per horizontal carousel page and keeps a Play all action.
- Music Videos for You uses wide 16:9 cards in a horizontal carousel, with a clear play affordance and title overlay.
- Your Playlists uses a Spotify-style two-column tile layout when there are **four or more** saved playlists. With **three or fewer**, it keeps the existing horizontal cover carousel.
- Albums for You appears immediately after Your Playlists.

### 4. Full-Screen Lyrics and Player Polish

- The lyrics compact header now uses a translate icon with an explicit **Romaji** or **Original** label instead of the ambiguous ROM/ORIG abbreviations.
- The lyrics card, album-art flight, and expanded lyrics surface share transition progress and easing so the artwork moves from the player into the compact header as the sheet expands or closes.
- The compact lyrics header and Android status-bar region use a darker tint related to the lyrics card palette.
- Rounded sheet corners, top and bottom text boundaries, and soft edge blur keep lyrics from appearing clipped against the sheet edges.
- The lyrics screen continues to expose playback controls during expansion, with the artwork/header interaction returning to the full player.
- The app is locked to portrait orientation for the player and lyrics layout.

### 5. Track and Playlist Actions

- Home song rows retain the three-dot options menu for queue actions, Liked Songs, playlists, and artist navigation.
- Saved playlist tiles remain clickable and category views show only saved playlists with relevant tracks.
- Existing playback, library, download, and playlist behavior from v1.4.7 is retained.

## Local Recommendation Design

The flow is:

`playback and library signals → local taste profile → category-specific candidate pool → on-device ranking → Home shelves`

Aura stores small interaction counters locally and builds artist, genre, and album affinities from local Liked Songs and Recently Played data. The ranker also applies exact-track engagement scores. No personal listening profile is uploaded or synchronized by this implementation.

This release is a local recommender, not a trained neural model. Its advantages are immediate feedback, no model download, and no personalization server. Fresh YouTube Music candidates still come from online catalog searches. Offline, Aura can only recommend items already present in saved or cached data.

## Upgrade and Data

Install over v1.4.7 using the same signing key to preserve local favorites, playlists, downloads, recent listening, and recommendation signals. Recommendation ordering may initially be modest for a fresh installation and improves as the listener plays, completes, skips, and likes more tracks.
