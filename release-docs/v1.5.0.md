# Aura v1.5.0

Release Tags: [release, v1.5.0, android, kotlin, jetpack-compose, sticky-header, mood-backgrounds, navigation-transitions, music-videos, comments, sound-equalizer, artist-editorial]

---

## Overview

Aura v1.5.0 represents a monumental milestone in Aura's visual, architectural, and tactile evolution. This release introduces a seamless YouTube Music-inspired collapsing dynamic sticky header with shared continuous mood backgrounds, fluid directional horizontal slide screen transitions, native YouTube music video extraction and playback integration, in-app YouTube song comments sheet, rich artist editorial biographies, an interactive graphic equalizer with live audio wave animations, and refined typography and touch feedback across every surface.

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 8.0+ (API 26 to API 36), Aura v1.5.0 pushes Android UI engineering to flagship standards while retaining complete local-first recommendation privacy and zero cloud lock-in.

---

## Key Highlights & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Collapsing Dynamic Sticky Header & Shared Mood Backdrop

- **Continuous Atmospheric Canvas**: When selecting a category (e.g. *Energize*, *Relax*, *Workout*, *Focus*, *Commute*), a single continuous atmospheric image spans both the top sticky header region and the Quick Picks section, delivering an immersive, cohesive visual experience without seam artifacts.
- **Contextual Scroll Mechanics**:
  - **Un-tapped ("All") Home Screen**: The time-aware greeting banner and category capsules scroll naturally off-screen with the feed content, leaving only a sleek, transparent Android status bar pinned at the top.
  - **Category Selected**: Upon activating any category pill, the greeting smoothly collapses upward into the status bar, and the category pill carousel docks securely beneath the status bar as an edge-to-edge solid dark header.
- **Simultaneous Mood Background & Content Reveal**: While content for a newly tapped category is fetching over the network, the screen remains pure dark with smooth skeleton loading placeholders. Once data arrives, both the atmospheric mood image and the fresh shelf content crossfade in synchronously with an elegant `380ms EaseOutCubic` curve.
- **Curated Night & Sunset Mood Atmospheres**: Replaced high-glare daytime imagery with curated dusk, twilight, concert crowd, and starry night backdrops. Added an on-device `ColorMatrix` exposure filter (`setToScale(0.62f, 0.60f, 0.68f, 1f)`) to guarantee 100% text contrast and readability.
- **Selective Scrim Gradient**: The dark background fade at the bottom of Quick Picks is localized strictly to items 3 and 4, keeping items 1 and 2 fully bright and vibrant.
- **High-Contrast Dark Pills**: When docked against the darkened sticky header, unselected category pills adopt a refined charcoal gray fill (`#353945`) with crisp borders for effortless visual differentiation.

### 2. Category Pill Micro-Interactions (SimpMusic Inspired)

- **Sweeping Conic Border Indicator**: Loading pills render an infinite 360-degree rotating gradient sweep border along their capsule perimeter while background network queries resolve.
- **Tactile Scale Feedback**: Category capsules compress to `scale(0.97)` on touch down with zero latency, releasing smoothly into an expanded active badge.
- **Auto-Centering Scroll**: Selecting any category pill automatically animates that capsule toward the horizontal center of the viewport, maintaining optimal thumb reachability.
- **Instant Reset Transition**: Tapping the active pill again or tapping "All" smoothly unwinds the docked header back into the normal scrollable home feed.

### 3. Directional Horizontal Slide Navigation

- **Spatial Tab Navigation**: Screen transitions across the bottom navigation destinations (**Home**, **Search**, **Library**, **Settings**) now use directional horizontal sliding (`slideInHorizontally` + `slideOutHorizontally`) paired with a custom Apple-grade easing curve (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`).
- **Logical Directional Awareness**: Navigating forward through the tab bar slides content from right to left; stepping back slides from left to right.
- **Scroll State Preservation**: Wrapped in Compose `SaveableStateProvider`, ensuring scroll positions, list offsets, and active search states are fully preserved when switching between tabs.
- **Sheet & Detail Screen Flow**: Artist Details and Playlist Details screens incorporate matching horizontal entry and exit transitions for cohesive spatial continuity.

### 4. YouTube Music Video Extraction & In-App Playback

- **Direct Music Video Resolution**: `SimpMusicExtractorEngine` now resolves official YouTube music video streams (`MUSIC_VIDEO_TYPE_OMV`) alongside pure audio streams.
- **Contextual Playback Handling**: Seamlessly switches between high-bitrate audio and high-definition video playback depending on user selection.
- **Graceful Error Recovery**: `AuraPlayerManager` detects video unavailability or geographic stream restrictions and falls back gracefully to audio, notifying the user via discreet snackbars without interrupting listening sessions.
- **Artist Video Shelves**: Artist Detail Screen directly surfaces official music video carousels with 16:9 thumbnails and direct play actions.

### 5. In-App YouTube Comments Sheet

- **Native Community Conversation**: Added `AuraCommentsBottomSheet` and `AuraCommentsExtractor` allowing listeners to browse real-time top and recent YouTube comments directly inside the Now Playing sheet.
- **Tactile Drag Handle UX**: Equipped with a native rounded drag handle / grabber pill for gesture dismissal, eliminating cluttered close buttons for a modern sheet experience.
- **Author Badges & Formatted Timestamps**: Renders channel avatars, verified badges, like counts, and relative comment timestamps with full Dark Mode styling.

### 6. Artist Editorial Biographies & Profiles

- **Enriched Artist Knowledge**: Integrated `ArtistEditorialRepository` to parse and render rich editorial bios, formation dates, home countries, and genre tags on `ArtistDetailScreen`.
- **Wikipedia & MusicBrainz Fallbacks**: Automatically supplements YouTube Music metadata with rich descriptive artist narratives.

### 7. Sound Equalizer & Live Wave Visualizer

- **Graphic Equalizer UI**: Added `EqualizerWaveSection` featuring customizable band presets (Bass Boost, Vocal, Acoustic, Electronic, Flat) with real-time decibel gain sliders.
- **Live Waveform Playing Indicator**: Added `AudioPlayingIndicator` with multi-bar randomized rhythmic height animations reflecting active playback states.

### 8. Interactive Draggable Cover Art (Spotify Swipe-to-Skip)

- **1:1 Direct Manipulation Gesture**: The full-screen Now Playing album art is now directly draggable horizontally, following the user's touch with zero-lag translation.
- **Queue Artwork Previews**: Dragging left or right dynamically peeks the adjacent next or previous track's album art into view from the active playback queue.
- **Physical Feel & Rubber-Banding**: Includes subtle 4-degree dynamic rotational tilt, depth scaling, and boundary rubber-banding resistance at the ends of the queue.
- **Threshold & Spring Snap-Back**: Dragging past 72dp smoothly exits the card and triggers `onSkipNext` or `onSkipPrevious`; incomplete drags bounce back to center with a critically damped spring.
- **Smooth Track Entrance**: Advancing tracks enter with a fluid horizontal slide curve (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`).

### 9. Search, Library & Design Polish

- **Carousel Description Typography & Boundary Polish**: Resolved text clipping on horizontal carousels by eliminating container-level rounded corner clipping on Top Artists, Top Playlists, Recently Played, Your Playlists, and Albums for You. Expanded column widths and adjusted row heights so subscriber counts, uploader names, artist subtitles, and song counts render completely without corner cutoff.
- **Library Recently Added Section**: Replaced the static "Your tracks" section on the Library screen with **"Recently added"**, populating the list with the user's authentic recently played songs for instant replay and queueing.
- **Search Exploration Cards**: Updated the **Anime & J-Pop** category card search query to `"Anime Jpop"` for high-relevance catalog curation.
- **Quick Picks High Legibility**: Elevated Quick Picks artist and metadata subtitle text color to crisp `#FFFFFF` for enhanced readability over dark scrims.
- **Pull-To-Refresh**: Native pull-to-refresh on `HomeScreen` to effortlessly re-query recommendations and reset carousel states.
- **Playlist Duplicate Protection**: `PlaylistDetailsScreen` prevents accidental duplicate track additions while offering explicit override prompts.
- **Long-Press Context Menus**: Extended long-press contextual menus to Search Screen recent history and Home recommendation items.

## Upgrade and Migration Notes

- **Seamless Data Continuity**: Upgrading from v1.4.8 or earlier preserves all local playlists, liked songs, downloaded tracks, listening history, and recommendation taste profiles.
- **Local Cache**: Existing image caches remain valid; new curated mood backdrops and comments fetch on-demand and cache in app-scoped internal storage.
